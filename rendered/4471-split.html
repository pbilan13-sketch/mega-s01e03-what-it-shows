How each report was placed

  1. One id, one report. The ticket export comes in overlapping pages, so some reports appear twice. Count unique ids (uniqueById), never rows.
  2. The tenant decides, not intake. The intake tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser. The cause comes from the tenant: its locale (day first or not) and its booking form (causes.ts). Intake’s tags disagree with the tenant’s cause in both directions.
  3. Read the outliers. A few reports are not a day-month swap: the month stays, the day moves by one (“Lisbon got Tuesday, Toronto got Monday”). The desk noted them (desk-notes.json) and files them under their tenant’s cause, so they count there. They are tracked in their own ticket, because neither the parser fix nor retiring the form explains a one-day move.

The problems

  • parser: the shared date parser read every date month first. Every day-first tenant was hit, through every channel. Fixed.
  • hint: booking-v3 printed MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People at day-first tenants typed what the hint said, and the form read it day first. Only form reports from booking-v3 tenants. Won’t fix: the form is retired.
  • one-day: the date keeps its month and slides by a day, depending on where the invitee is: a time-zone / DST conversion. Not in the engineering update. If one-day reports keep coming after the parser fix, this is the ticket to reopen.
